What is a Payment Automation?

Bd payment automation is a type of technological solution that allows merchants to easily accept payments online. It acts as a medium between buyers and sellers and encrypts payment information and sends it securely to the server. It is used to accept payments on various online platforms including digital services. Several other payment automations are popular in Bangladesh, including zinipay. Our site is the best payment automation in Bangladesh.

How it works :

  1. 01

    Payment automation bd is of two types and works in several steps.

  2. 02

    First, the buyer selects his preferred method and provides his payment details such as card, bkash payment automation, mobile wallet number or bank details.

  3. 03

    After this, if the payment type is selected, the online payment automation in Bangladesh directly collects it by encrypting it and sends it to the bank or related financial institution.

  4. 04

    After getting approval from the bank, the money is deposited in the merchant's account like bkash payment automation.

  5. 05

    And if the payment type is personal number, then the payment details from the buyer's phone are stored on the server, and the payment will be completed when the customer inputs the transaction ID of the payment sent by him on the bd payment automation page and verifies it.

  6. 06

    These internal tasks are completed very quickly, resulting in a better payment experience for the buyer.
